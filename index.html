What this is

Meat and seafood, grown, not raised

Cultivated (or "lab-grown") meat is animal muscle and fat tissue, grown from a small sample of living cells in a steel bioreactor instead of on a farm. The cells are fed nutrients so they multiply and form tissue, which is then harvested and shaped into cuts or blended with plant-based ingredients.

Behind the map

Who makes it, and where it's produced

A handful of companies grow the cultivated meat featured on this map. Here's who they are and where their production facilities are.

1

Wildtype — salmon

San Francisco, CA. Produced at "the Fishery," a converted microbrewery in Dogpatch.

2

GOOD Meat (Eat Just) — chicken

Alameda, CA (demo plant), plus a large-scale facility in Singapore.

3

Upside Foods — chicken

Emeryville, CA (EPIC facility), plus a newer plant in Glenview, IL.

4

SuperMeat — chicken

Ness Ziona, Israel. Non-commercial pilot plant next to its test restaurant.

5

Believer Meats — chicken (shut down)

Built a large plant in Wilson, NC and won USDA approval in 2025, then ceased operations that year after a lawsuit, before reaching the market.

6

Mission Barns — pork fat

San Francisco, CA (cell cultivation) plus a Berkeley, CA processing site. First company cleared to sell cultivated pork anywhere; its cultivated fat is blended with plant protein into meatballs and bacon.

Sources: company sites, Green Queen, Food Dive, AgFunderNews.
